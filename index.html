About

How I approach design

I'm Shyamu Baghel, a graphic designer with 5+ years of experience who turns briefs into visuals that actually perform — on a shelf, a hoarding, a magazine page, or a feed. Over the last few years I've built a strong, hands-on track record working closely with a leading national hardware brand, shaping how it looks across print, packaging and digital.

I bring more than just software skills to the table — a sharp eye for detail, genuine passion for visual storytelling, and the discipline to take a project from a rough idea to a print-ready, production-perfect file. Clients and teams I've worked with know me as someone who delivers fast, delivers clean, and never compromises on quality — the kind of designer who makes brands look bigger and more premium than their budget suggests.
